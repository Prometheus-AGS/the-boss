# Teams in Work

## Why
Team execution is presently accessible through administration, while the approved first customer delivery requires coding teams in Work without manual JSON or seeded records.

## What Changes
- Work entrypoint for selecting a coding team, workspace and natural-language task.
- Reuse durable UAR catalog/instance/execution/approval contracts and show members, outputs, artifacts, cancellation and persisted reopening.
- Add a usable coding preset through normal catalog deployment, complete translations, and a packaged operation procedure.

## Impact
Runtime/IPC/preload contracts, renderer Work navigation and locales; no new scheduler or duplicate runtime. Parent scope C14.1/C14.2 and coding portions of C16.1/C16.2 remain pending until their full criteria are satisfied.
