## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Task-specific bounded starting points
The authoring editor SHALL offer product research, marketing/brand, logo/design, mobile design and customer-feedback presets alongside the existing presets. Each SHALL declare responsibilities, outputs and evidence with a separate reviewer, preserving explicit model, skill, knowledge and tool choices through immutable save/deploy/reopen.

#### Scenario: Create and revise a practical team
- **WHEN** the operator chooses a practical preset and saves explicit resources and delivery instructions
- **THEN** the existing compiler produces a new immutable package identity and reopening restores the same draft resources
- **AND** revising delivery instructions produces a new identity without mutating the prior revision or run

### Requirement: Artifacts do not confer connector authority
Practical presets SHALL bound work to requested artifacts and project scope. Customer-feedback SHALL prepare feedback and issue drafts and record connector requirements without issuing GitHub effects. Role names, scope text and supplied research SHALL NOT grant tool authority.

#### Scenario: Feedback needs a repository action
- **WHEN** a customer-feedback team receives feedback that could become a GitHub issue
- **THEN** its output identifies draft content and required repository, connector and action permissions
- **AND** it does not claim an issue was created or send/publish without separate authorization

### Requirement: Real operation evidence
Acceptance SHALL use a packaged visible editor/Work operation with real product, marketing and design member attempts, exact model receipts, explicit reviewed skill pins, scoped readonly filesystem approvals and correlated artifacts. Saved identities and pinned runs SHALL survive reopening without repeating inference.

#### Scenario: Operate representative practical teams
- **WHEN** the completed package runs representative practical teams against a disposable brief
- **THEN** artifacts identify actual member/task/attempt identities and evidence
- **AND** evidence distinguishes source preparation, observed execution and remaining platform/connector gaps
